🔒 Privacy & Data Policy

What We Collect

When you upload a PDF, we store the file temporarily on our server along with your chosen print settings. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

How We Use Your Files

Your uploaded file is used solely for the purpose of printing your document. We do not read, analyse, or share the contents of your files with any third party.

File Retention

Uploaded files are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours after your print job is completed or cancelled. Unclaimed jobs are deleted after 7 days.

Security

All file uploads are transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted connection). Our servers use industry-standard security practices to protect your data while it is stored.

Your Rights

You may request immediate deletion of your uploaded file at any time by contacting us at the counter or via WhatsApp. We will promptly remove your data upon request.
